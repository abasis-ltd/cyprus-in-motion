Expo kit · Cyprus in Motion

Everything for the stand in one folder. It all runs offline from this folder on a Mac — the website and the game are also online, so visitors can open them on their phones from the QR codes.

01 · BIG SCREEN

The expo show

A 9-minute looping presentation: all 12 projects on a live map of every bus line in Cyprus, with real product captures. It never stops and needs no clicks.

  • Mac: double-click Start show (kiosk).command
  • Any TV with USB: play media/cyprus-in-motion-show-1080p.mp4 (in the offline kit, or download it below) on repeat
  • Keys: Space pause · ← → scenes · 1–6 chapters · F full screen
Open the showDownload MP4 ↗
02 · TABLET / SECOND SCREEN

Beat the Bus

Visitors guess when five real Nicosia buses arrived and try to beat the official timetable. Booth mode shows today’s leaderboard and resets after 60 s idle.

  • Mac: double-click Start game (kiosk).command
  • Tablet: open play/?booth=1 from the website and add it to the home screen
